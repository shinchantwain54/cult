I — 魔道 · THE DEMONIC PATH

Strength Is the
Only Scripture

The Orthodox sects of the Murim teach restraint: temper desire, bow to heaven, cultivate virtue and let righteousness guide the sword. The Demonic Path rejects every word of it.

To walk 魔道 is to believe that heaven is indifferent and that morality is a leash forged by the weak. Qi is not purified but devoured, desire is not suppressed but wielded, and the self is the only master a martial artist must answer to.

STRENGTH

SELF

No heaven above the will. The disciple's own Dao is law.

II — 神敎 · THE DIVINE CULT

An Empire in the
Hundred Thousand Mountains

Far beyond the Central Plains, in the mist-choked peaks of the Hundred Thousand Great Mountains, the Cult rules a hidden domain of fortresses, forges and training grounds carved into black rock. The Murim Alliance has marched on it more than once. The mountains are still full of their bones.

III — 天魔九律 · THE DOCTRINES

The Nine Laws of
the Heavenly Demon

The Nine Laws are carved into the black stone of the Hall of Ten Thousand Demons, and every disciple recites them at dawn. Elders may argue over technique and succession. No one argues over these.

    天魔

    Cheonma,
    The Heavenly Demon

    Cheonma is not a name but a title, worn by the one who stands at the summit of the Demonic Path. Each Heavenly Demon earns the throne alone. Whoever masters the Heavenly Demon Divine Art and outlives every rival becomes, to the faithful, a living god.

    Orthodox chronicles call each one a calamity. The faithful call them salvation. Both agree on one thing: when the Heavenly Demon descends from the mountains, the Murim bleeds.

    "Heaven did not give me this throne. I took it, and heaven was silent."
    V — 魔功 · DEMONIC ARTS

    The Forbidden Techniques

    Demonic arts give power faster than any Orthodox method, and they cost more. Many who attempt them suffer qi deviation and go mad. Those who survive become monsters.

    SUPREME · SOVEREIGN ONLY

    Heavenly Demon Divine Art

    The founding art of the Cult. It turns the user's qi into a black-crimson flame that consumes other energy and finally the boundary between human and demon.

    GRAND · INNER SANCTUM

    Heaven-Shattering Palm

    A single palm thrust that bursts the meridians of everyone in its path. Mountainsides have been found split by its echo.

    FORBIDDEN · BLOOD HALL

    Blood Demon Refinement

    Draws strength from the blood of fallen foes. Every battle makes the practitioner stronger and less human.

    SECRET · SHADOW GUARD

    Phantom Demon Steps

    Footwork that leaves afterimages of the user. By the time the enemy strikes, the real body is behind them.

    GRAND · BLACK BANNER

    Nine Hells Sword

    Nine forms, each colder than the last. Its final form is said to freeze the blood in a man's veins before the blade lands.

    CORE · ALL DISCIPLES

    Demon Heart Method

    The foundation every believer learns: letting rage and desire flow into the dantian instead of fighting them.
